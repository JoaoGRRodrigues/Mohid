# Compound sum

- 

$$
\\sum^{(p)} = \\underbrace{ \\sum \\circ \\sum \\circ \\ldots \\circ \\sum }_{p-\\text{times}}
$$

,
- 

$$
\\sum^{(0)} = id
$$

,
- 

$$
\\sum^{n,\\,(p)}_{\\alpha_{i+1}=1,\\,(i)} = \\underbrace{ \\sum^{n}_{\\alpha_2=1} \\circ \\sum^{n}_{\\alpha_3=1} \\circ \\ldots \\circ \\sum^{n}_{\\alpha_{p+1}=1} }_{p-\\text{times}}
$$
