# Calendar

[Mohid](../../overview/mohid.md) (un)official [calendar](http://www.google.com/calendar).

Mohiders may subscribe to this calendar using their google account. Otherwise they can always create a google account in order to subscribe it. Some interesting features of google calendar are **free sms reminders**, email reminders and group meeting email invitations.
